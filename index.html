Nol-Corp LLC buys residential property and land for its own portfolio. You deal directly with the buyer. Depending on the property and your goals, we can also discuss seller financing, where you receive payments over time instead of one lump sum at closing.

Every property and seller is different. We start with a conversation, then put any proposal in writing for you to review.

Where we buy: Irvine (including Woodbridge, Northwood, Turtle Rock, University Park, Great Park, and Portola Springs), nearby Orange County cities such as Tustin, Newport Beach, Lake Forest, and Costa Mesa, and selectively in other markets.

This is not an offer to purchase any specific property. Any offer depends on our review of the property and on terms agreed in writing. Seller financing isn't right for every situation, so please talk with your own attorney, tax advisor, or financial advisor before you agree to terms.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you buy homes and land directly?

Yes. Nol-Corp LLC buys homes and land directly from owners for its own portfolio. Share some details about your property and we'll tell you whether it's a fit and what terms could work.

What is seller financing?

With seller financing, you as the seller are paid over time under agreed terms instead of receiving the full price at closing. It can work well for some sellers and not for others. We'll explain how it would work for your property, and we recommend reviewing it with your own advisors.

Do you buy outside Irvine and Orange County?

Our main focus is Irvine and nearby Orange County, but we consider homes and land in other markets case by case. Get in touch either way.

What kinds of digital assets do you buy?

Domain names, websites, and other digital properties. Email us the details to get started.
